# 06 — Revenue Model

> **Two streams now, three by Year 2.** The streams are designed so that customers of one create the data exhaust that powers the others.

---

## Revenue Streams

### Stream 1: SaaS Subscriptions (Year 1, primary)

**Customer:** Active US debt buyers, collection agencies, debt brokers.
**ICP cuts:** Buyers doing $1M–$50M/yr in face-value purchases. Big enough to pay; small enough to need help.

**Tiers:**

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | $99/mo | Deal Radar, 2 portfolio scores/mo, email alerts |
| **Pro** | $299/mo | Unlimited radar, 10 scores/mo, MCP API access, compliance tracker |
| **Team** | $899/mo | All Pro + 5 seats + $1k research credit + priority support |
| **Enterprise** | Custom $2k–$5k/mo | Custom integrations, white-label MCP, dedicated success |

**Why these prices:**
- Anchored to existing market: RMAI dues alone are $1,500–$5,000/yr. Compliance tracker subscriptions run $1k–$5k/yr.
- Pro tier ($299) is below the "ask my boss" threshold for most buyers — easier sale.
- Team tier captures multi-seat agencies.

### Stream 2: Research Reports (start in Phase 3)

**Customer:** Banks, debt brokers, institutional investors, regulators.
**Product:**
- **Quarterly Bank Divestiture Report** — $2,500 per issue, $7,500/yr subscription
- **Asset Class Deep Dives** (medical, auto, CC) — $5,000 each, 2/yr per class
- **Custom Research** (commissioned) — $5,000–$25,000 per project

**Why this works:** SaaS data exhaust → reports. By Phase 3 you have unique aggregated visibility no one else has. Banks pay for that view of *themselves and competitors.*

**Year 1 target:** 2 reports sold ($5k–$10k total)
**Year 2 target:** 12 reports + 5 quarterly subs (~$60k–$80k)

### Stream 3: Licensed Buying (Year 2)

Detailed in `03_PHASE_ROADMAP.md` Phase 4–5. Not a Phase 1 revenue stream. Funded by Streams 1+2.

## Unit Economics (SaaS)

**Assumptions (conservative):**
- CAC: $0–$200 in Phase 1 (founder-led sales, content, conferences)
- Average MRR per customer: $200 (blend of Solo + Pro)
- Gross margin: ~85% (cloud infra is the only material variable cost)
- Churn: 5–8% monthly initially → 3% steady state
- LTV at 5% churn: $200 / 0.05 = $4,000

**Path to $5k MRR:**
- 25 customers @ avg $200 = $5,000 MRR
- At 5–10 conversions per month from Phase 2 onward, this lands by ~Week 26.

## Path to $5k MRR (Sales Math)

| Funnel stage | Monthly volume needed (steady state) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Trial signups | 30 | List, conferences, content |
| Active design partners → trials | ~15 | Conversion from list |
| Paid conversions | 5 | 33% trial→paid |
| Net new customers (after churn) | 4 | 5 minus ~1 churn |

**At net 4/mo, you reach 25 customers in ~6 months.**

**Acquisition channels (in priority order):**
1. **Direct outreach** to RMAI/ACA member directories — highest conversion
2. **Industry conferences** (RMAI annual, Insidearm Receivables Roundup) — sponsor or speak
3. **LinkedIn newsletter** — weekly market commentary, drives inbound
4. **Partner referrals** (debt brokers, attorneys) — 10% recurring affiliate
5. **Content/SEO** — slow burn; long-tail queries on "[state] debt buyer license," "how to score a debt portfolio"

**Don't waste budget on:** generic Google/Facebook ads (audience too small, intent too weak) or PR firms.

## Pricing Tactics

- **Founder pricing for design partners:** 50% off forever for first 5 customers in exchange for testimonials, case studies, and product feedback
- **Annual discount:** 2 months free on annual prepay (improves cash flow + reduces churn)
- **Money-back guarantee for first 30 days:** removes friction; almost no one claims it
- **Don't haggle on Solo tier;** customize Enterprise as needed

---

## Kill / Pivot Criteria (Per Stream)

### SaaS
- 🔴 **Kill:** <$300 MRR after 6 months of trying with 50+ qualified outreach
- 🟡 **Pivot:** $300–$1,000 MRR but high churn → fix churn before adding features
- 🟢 **Double down:** $1,000+ MRR with <10% churn → hire fractional sales help

### Research Reports
- 🔴 **Kill:** No report sold by Month 12
- 🟡 **Pivot:** Weak demand → reposition as bundled with Enterprise tier
- 🟢 **Double down:** 3+ paid in Year 1 → quarterly cadence + dedicated researcher

### Licensed Buying (Year 2 gate)
- 🔴 **Don't start** unless: SaaS $5k+ MRR + $25k+ war chest + state license issued + servicing partner identified
- Even then, first portfolio < $15k purchase price. Walk before run.

## Cost Discipline Rules

1. **No paid hire** until $5k MRR
2. **No paid ads** until you've exhausted free direct outreach
3. **No conference sponsorship** above $1k until $3k MRR
4. **No tool over $50/mo** until it directly enables a paid customer
5. **Founder salary: $0** until $10k MRR (eat from main job / savings)

## What Could Go Wrong (Honest Section)

| Scenario | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market is smaller than thought | Medium | High | Phase 0 calls de-risk this |
| Buyers won't pay for SaaS, want commission deals | Medium | Medium | Have an affiliate-style fallback ready |
| Established competitor copies you | Medium | Medium | MCP-native + speed of iteration; data moat over time |
| FCRA/CFPB enforcement of edge cases | Low-Medium | Critical | Conservative architecture + attorney review |
| Apify shuts down free tier or pricing 5x | Medium | Low | Self-hosted Playwright fallback already in stack |
| You burn out solo | High over time | High | Phase gates explicitly include "ops runnable in 15hr/wk" |

## North Star Metric

**Weekly Active Buyers** — customers who logged in or hit the API at least once in 7 days.

Track this from Day 1. If it ever drops below 70% of paid customers, retention is the priority over growth.

## Final Note on the Original Vision

You came in with three intuitions:
1. **Buy debt cheap, restructure, leverage** ← right strategy, wrong sequencing for a $5k start
2. **Automate finding buyers/sellers** ← right; this is what we're building
3. **Sell data to other buyers** ← right pattern, wrong unit (sell aggregated/B2B insights, not consumer profiles)

This roadmap keeps everything you wanted; it just orders the moves so you don't get wiped out by capital, regulation, or both. Phase 1 is software where the rules are kinder. Phase 5 is where the original "buy debt, leverage" vision actually executes — with cash flow, relationships, and a team behind it instead of a $5k bet.
